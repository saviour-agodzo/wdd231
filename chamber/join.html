NP Membership Benefits

Nonprofit organizations can join at no cost and receive:

  • A basic listing in the chamber directory
  • Invitations to community networking events
  • Access to chamber announcements and resources

Bronze Membership Benefits

Bronze members receive NP benefits plus:

  • Enhanced directory listing
  • Member networking invitations
  • Quarterly business profile in the chamber newsletter

Silver Membership Benefits

Silver members receive Bronze benefits plus:

  • Discounts on selected chamber events
  • Reduced pricing for business training sessions
  • Eligibility for a rotating home page spotlight

Gold Membership Benefits

Gold members receive Silver benefits plus:

  • Priority home page spotlight placement
  • Preferred sponsorship opportunities
  • Invitations to exclusive member roundtables
